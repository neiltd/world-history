Privacy

This site sets no cookies.

This site uses no analytics.

It keeps one flag in your browser's session storage, so that the opening plays once per visit; it holds only that the opening has played, and it is gone when you close the tab.

The site is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps its own server logs, which can include your IP address, under its own privacy statement.

If you send a report, this is what happens. A report sends what you type, plus the web address of the page, the Claim or Source you were looking at, the version of the site, and whether you were in Explore or Research. A name and an email are optional.

This report goes by email to the site's editor through FormSubmit. It is not public. FormSubmit says it keeps submissions for 30 days. On the next page you may be asked to tick a box. That page is run by FormSubmit and uses Google reCAPTCHA, which can set a Google cookie and sees your IP address. This site does not. The editor keeps your report until it has been dealt with and for 90 days after, then deletes it. To remove your credit or your data, choose "Remove my credit or my data" in the first question of the form, and say in "Anything else" which preferred name or email. Please do not include anyone else's personal details. A person reads the reports we receive. We aim to reply within a week if you left an email. Checking a Source can take weeks, because we read it ourselves, through a library. What you report cannot change the page by itself; only a Source we have read can. This is not a vote, and it is not the place to add a fact.

To remove your credit or your data, open any "Question this Claim" or "Know this Source?" control and choose "Remove my credit or my data". Say in "Anything else" which preferred name or email to remove.
